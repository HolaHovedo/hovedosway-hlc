What is coaching, exactly?

Coaching is a structured partnership focused on where you are going, not on diagnosing where you have been. I am not here to hand you advice or a plan off a shelf — I ask the questions that help you see your own patterns, then we build small, sustainable steps and I hold you to them.

How is this different from therapy?

Therapy generally treats mental health conditions and often works with the past. Coaching works forward from where you are now, with a healthy client, toward specific change. They complement each other well, and plenty of people do both. Coaching is not therapy and is not a substitute for it.

How do I know if coaching is right for me?

If you already know roughly what you want to change but keep not doing it, coaching is likely a good fit. If you are in crisis, or dealing with something that needs clinical care, therapy or medical care should come first — and I will say so plainly rather than take you on.
