Life Coaching · My own program

The Art of Being Liked

by YOURSELF and others

Likability is what happens when you stop performing for approval. Learning to like yourself is the prerequisite to being genuinely liked by others.

Twelve weeks, three phases. It moves deliberately — you spend the first month entirely on your relationship with yourself before we touch anything relational, because the second half does not hold without the first.

Phase One · Weeks 1–4

By Yourself — Seeing & Accepting

The foundation. You learn to see yourself clearly, name your patterns, own them without shame, and make peace with what you find. Nothing here is about other people yet — this is the YOURSELF the title capitalizes.

Week 01

Self Reflection

Build an honest baseline picture of your relationship with yourself before any change work begins. We start by noticing, not fixing.

Week 02

Identification

Move from general seeing to specific naming. You identify your recurring approval-seeking loops — and locate the fear underneath them.

Week 03

Admission

Own what you have seen, without judgment or shame. The pivot from observing a pattern to honestly saying: yes, this is mine.

Week 04

Self Acceptance

Make peace with what has been seen and admitted. Self-compassion becomes the emotional ground everything after this is built on.

Phase Two · Weeks 5–11

And Others — Showing Up & Connecting

You take the self you have accepted and bring it into contact with other people: expressing it honestly, anchoring it in values, and building the relational skills that make connection possible without self-abandonment.

Week 05

Authentic Self-Expression

Begin saying what is true — your desires, opinions and feelings — without pre-editing every sentence for approval.

Week 06

Values & Identity

Anchor your expression in something steadier than mood: your values and your chosen identity. This is what makes likability self-sourced.

Week 07

Presence & Attention

Shift from self-focus to genuine presence with others. Being with people without performing — attention itself becomes the connective skill.

Week 08

Empathy & Attunement

Get onto another person’s map and make them feel genuinely understood. The relational heart of being liked by others.

Week 09

Boundaries

The people-pleaser antidote. Holding your own line is the proof that connection no longer requires abandoning yourself.

Week 10

Resilience to Disapproval

Non-neediness. You build tolerance for being disliked, which frees likability to become a byproduct rather than a goal.

Week 11

Integration & Habit Anchoring

Turn ten weeks of practice into durable habits. This is where change stops depending on motivation and starts running on its own.

Phase Three · Week 12

The Return

Week 12

Renewed Self Acceptance

Close the spiral. You return to self-acceptance enriched by the whole journey — same ground, new altitude — with an ongoing practice that carries the work past the program.

Health Coaching

Built around what you are actually trying to change

Health Coaching here draws on tools and methods I have built through years of research and coursework in becoming a certified Health and Life Coach — combined with my own lived experience putting them into practice. We work across the areas that actually move the needle: nutrition, movement, mental and physical health, and the daily habits that hold them together.

No fixed length

We work at the pace that fits your goals. Some people need a few sessions to build a habit that sticks; others want ongoing support.

One-hour sessions

Weekly, scheduled the week before, however works for you — phone, Zoom, FaceTime, Google Meet, WhatsApp.

No condition required

You do not need a specific health condition to benefit. This is goal-specific coaching, built around what you want to change.

Specialty Focus Within Health Coaching

Diabetes Focus

I live with diabetes myself, which is why it is a specialty focus here — not a credential on a wall, but lived experience. I know the blood sugar rollercoaster from the inside, and I coach using tools that target it directly: water intake, blood-sugar-focused exercise, and the daily habits that actually move the needle. Sessions run one hour, at whatever pace fits your goals.
